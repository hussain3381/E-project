## 📋 Problem Statement & Objectives

[cite_start]OceanGate Limited is a 3-year-old enterprise dealing in diverse data storage solutions, ranging from classic optical storage media to modern high-performance solid-state tiers[cite: 22]. 

[cite_start]As demand escalated across both local and international markets, the company required a web platform to solve key business limitations[cite: 24, 30]:
1. [cite_start]**Product Specification & Discovery:** Allowing prospective customers to view overall looks, features, and precise storage techniques[cite: 25].
2. [cite_start]**Comparison System:** Enabling users to dynamically evaluate products within similar or different brand categories (e.g., 24X vs 52X drive speeds)[cite: 26, 43].
3. [cite_start]**Information Flow Optimization:** Minimizing delivery mismatches by making the entire product lineup transparent before ordering[cite: 27].
4. [cite_start]**Direct Technical Support:** Bypassing busy physical telephone lines with structured support addresses and direct service center maps[cite: 28, 33].

## ✨ Features Implemented (Requirement Mapping)

[cite_start]Based on the hired storyboards and customer requirement specifications, the following components were built[cite: 31, 50]:

- [cite_start]**Home Console:** Styled with an immersive dark cyberpunk theme, proper sections, responsive layout configurations, and a unique custom logo[cite: 40].
- [cite_start]**Dynamic Full-Width Promo Slider:** A native Bootstrap 5 carousel system with absolute text overlays (`z-index: 30`) displaying live offers and new arrivals safely[cite: 52].
- [cite_start]**Categorized Shop Engine:** Filters storage products according to specific brand lines and architectural specifications[cite: 32, 41].
- [cite_start]**Brand Comparison Grid:** Allows parallel product tracking so users don't waste operational resources due to unexpected product scaling[cite: 27, 43].
- [cite_start]**About Us & Journey Module:** Provides information on the company's 3-year history, client base, and high-performance engineering vision[cite: 22, 35].
- [cite_start]**Contact & Support Portal:** Includes official service network contacts and an active mail link that invokes local mail clients seamlessly for immediate support inquiries[cite: 33, 44].
